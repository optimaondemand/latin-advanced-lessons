Deponents in the Present System

📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Good news: you already know these endings.
A deponent's present, imperfect, and future are built exactly like the passives you learned last year — same stem, same markers, same endings. Nothing new to memorize. Only the translation changes: everything comes out active.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to produce the present, imperfect, and future of loquor in all six persons.
2
I will be able to conjugate a 1st-conjugation deponent (hortor) and see the same pattern at work.
3
I will be able to tell loqueris from loquēris and say why the macron matters.
📚 PART 1 · PRESENT TENSE — LOQUOR

Same passive endings you know (-or, -ris, -tur, -mur, -minī, -ntur), 3rd-conjugation vowels, active meanings:

SingularPlural
1stloquor — I speakloquimur — we speak
2ndloqueris — you speakloquiminī — you (pl.) speak
3rdloquitur — he/she/it speaksloquuntur — they speak
🎵 MEMORY WORK · SAY IT ALOUD THREE TIMES
loquor, loqueris, loquitur —
loquimur, loquiminī, loquuntur

Say it out loud. This chant is the same rhythm as the passive chants you already learned, and having it automatic is what lets you read Sallust at speed later this week.

📚 PART 2 · IMPERFECT AND FUTURE

Imperfect — the -bā- marker, exactly as before

SingularPlural
1stloquēbar — I was speakingloquēbāmur — we were speaking
2ndloquēbāris — you were speakingloquēbāminī — you (pl.) were speaking
3rdloquēbātur — he/she was speakingloquēbantur — they were speaking

Future — 3rd conjugation, so -a- then -ē-

SingularPlural
1stloquar — I will speakloquēmur — we will speak
2ndloquēris — you will speakloquēminī — you (pl.) will speak
3rdloquētur — he/she will speakloquentur — they will speak
⚠️ One macron, two tenses. Look carefully:
loqueris
short e → present: "you speak"
loquēris
long ē → future: "you will speak"
This is the same trap as regit / reget from Module 0, one step harder. In real Roman manuscripts the macron is not written — readers relied on context. You have macrons; use them.
📚 PART 3 · A 1ST-CONJUGATION DEPONENT: HORTOR

Change the conjugation and nothing about the system changes — only the stem vowel and the future marker, exactly as with ordinary verbs. Hortor, hortārī, hortātus sum — "to urge, encourage":

Tense3rd singular3rd plural
Presenthortātur — he urgeshortantur — they urge
Imperfecthortābātur — he was urginghortābantur — they were urging
Futurehortābitur — he will urgehortābuntur — they will urge
💡 Because it is 1st conjugation, the future uses -bi- (hortābitur) — just like laudābitur. Third-conjugation loquor uses -ē- (loquētur) — just like regētur. You are not learning new machinery. You are learning to read familiar machinery in the opposite direction.
