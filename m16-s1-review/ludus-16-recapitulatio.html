Ludus XVI
Recapitulātiō
M/J Latin Advanced · Module 16
Recapitulātiō — “a running back over the whole thing.” The Romans used it for the part of a speech where an orator gathers up everything he has said. This one gathers up the whole semester: forms, deponents, the gerundive, all four subjunctive tenses, sequence, every construction, the conditions and the irregulars. Nothing is labelled and nothing is grouped.
